How it works

The AI learned this game from zero with reinforcement learning, playing tens of thousands of rounds against its own past versions. Each version is a small neural network that gets 70 numbers about the game and answers 15 times a second with the buttons it presses. It runs in your browser: nothing is sent to a server. Privacy
